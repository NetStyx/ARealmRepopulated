## Functional Changes
- A looping scenario which instantaneously finishes will now be automatically detected and forced to wait an extra second between its runs. A notification is displayed when this happens.

## Bugfixes
- Actors now say the voice line of an emote they play right after appearing.
- A scenario imported from the clipboard is now upgraded when it was exported with an older version, the same way scenario files are.
- Saving in the scenario editor no longer undoes switching the scenario on or off in the list while the editor was open.
- The checkbox for switching a scenario on or off in the list no longer flickers back for a moment.
