# Face Value

Your actors finally get a mirror. Change their clan, hair, face and clothes right in the editor, or let the locals of an area inspire a whole new stranger.

## Functional Changes
- The "NPC Model" tab is now a full character editor. Pick the clan, gender and body type, and change everything the character creator offers, with the real color palettes and the labels that fit the clan.
- The "NPC Equipment" tab now lets you change every gear slot, the weapons and the facewear, and set both dyes. Gear only NPCs wear is listed too, by its model number.
- "Hide Weapons" and "Hide Headgear" moved to the "NPC Equipment" tab. They are joined by "Close visor" and, for Viera, "Hide ears".
- Faces and hairstyles also offer what NPCs of the clan wear beyond the character creator, on a tab of their own.
- Every color now has a free color picker next to its palette, for colors the character creator doesnt offer. Lip colors also get an opacity, and skin colors a muscle tone. Clicking a palette color undoes it.
- Click the eye icon at the top of an actor and a preview actor stands in front of you while you edit. It follows your changes after a moment.
- "Randomize" creates a stranger. With your current location as the "NPC source", they fit the area you are in, with the clans and clothes of the people who live there. Lock what you like, randomize again, and only the rest changes.
- "Pick from preset" is now "Pick from all NPCs". It lists the battle NPCs together with every named NPC of the game, so you can copy anyones look without traveling to them.

## Bugfixes
- Actors picked from a battle NPC preset now get the right mouth, lip color and eye colors. Actors you already picked keep their old look until you pick them again.
