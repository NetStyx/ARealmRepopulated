# Mirror, Mirror On The Wall

Some random actor woke up and decided that he does not want to look like Tataru anymore. Fair enough.

## Functional Changes
- The "NPC Model" tab is now a full character editor. Pick the clan, gender and body type, and change everything the character creator offers.
- The "NPC Equipment" tab now lets you change every gear slot including the dyes. Gear only NPCs wear is listed too ... but dont expect it to work with every model.
- "Hide Weapons" and "Hide Headgear" moved to the "NPC Equipment" tab. They are joined by "Close visor" and, for Viera, "Hide ears".
- Faces and hairstyles also offer what NPCs of the clan wear beyond the character creator, on a tab of their own.
- Click the eye icon at the top of an actor and a preview actor stands model for you while you edit.
- "Randomize" creates a new actor design for you. With your current location as the "NPC source", they fit the area you are in, with the clans and clothes of the people who live there. Lock what you like, randomize again, and only the rest changes.
- "Pick from preset" is now "Pick from all NPCs". It lists the battle NPCs together with every named NPC of the game, so you can copy anyones look without traveling to them.

## Bugfixes
- Actors picked from a battle NPC preset now get the right mouth, lip color and eye colors. Actors you already picked keep their old look until you pick them again.
