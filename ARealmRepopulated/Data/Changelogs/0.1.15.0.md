## Functional Changes
- Added "Characters" as option to group your actor designs together. Want to share a certain appearance or actor across scenarios? Now you can. This lets you design a look once and use it in as many scenarios as you like.
- The "General" tab of an actor has a "Character" picker. A linked actor takes name and look from its character, and every change to the character shows up in each scenario that uses it.
- "Save as character" turns the look of an actor into a new character, stored together with the scenario, and "Unlink" turns a linked actor back into an independent one that keeps its current look. Deleting a character does the same for every actor linked to it. Until the scenario is saved, a changed link shows "Unsaved changes".
