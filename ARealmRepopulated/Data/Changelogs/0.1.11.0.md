# Tidy Ranks

Your scenarios can now move into folders of their own. Actors who waited forever also learned to notice when they overstayed their welcome.

## Functional Changes
- Scenarios can now be sorted into groups with the new "Group" field in the scenario settings. The scenario list shows each group as a folder, and a / in the name nests one group in another.

## Technical Changes
- Chairs and beds are now recognized through the shared game definitions the plugin already relies on elsewhere, instead of a copy of its own, so seating holds up better across game updates.
- Restructured how actors step through their actions to make each step easier to follow.

## Bugfixes
- Actors that had actions which prompted them to wait indefinitly now fade out once the conditions of their scenario are no longer met. Unstable conditions like "Chance" are not considered for this.
- An actor whose actions are all turned off now stays put, the same as an actor without any actions. The other actors in its scenario wait for it at their first "Sync" action, instead of the scenario starting over right away.
- An actor reaching a path without any points now skips it and carries on with its next action.
- An actor of the "Sea Wolf" tribe now gets its randomly generated name from the correct pool and no longer a male "Highlander" name.
