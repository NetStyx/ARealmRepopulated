# Help at Hand

You now find a quick start and a way to the wiki right inside the plugin. Your actors also learned to stay on the ground and dont lie about their height anymore.

## Functional Changes
- The "Changelog" tab is now called "Info". It opens on a short help page with a quick start and an "Open wiki" button.
- Actors settle onto whatever solid foundation they stand on at the start of every run. Turn off "Snap to surface" to keep an actor at the height you stored.
- You had two sliders governing the height of an actor. Out of these, the "height multiplier" slider is gone as both did virtually the same thing. Your saved scenarios are automatically migrated.

## Technical Changes
- Notes on how the game behaves moved out of the code comments into their own place on the repository.
- Restructured the actor initialization to keep the parts involved better separated.

## Bugfixes
- Actors placed slightly inside the ground keep standing on it in every run of a looping scenario, not just the first.
- The "Height" setting in an actors appearance now accurately changes its size.
